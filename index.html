31 July 2026

Lab Wins Two Awards at 2026 Bionic Design Competition

#awards #moth #mudskipper #robot

The lab received two awards in the College & Youth Innovation category (大專暨青創組) at the 2026 Bionic Design Competition (仿生設計競賽). The mudskipper-inspired design received the Marine Sustainability Innovation Award (海洋永續創新獎) from the Ocean Conservation Administration, Ocean Affairs Council, while the silkmoth-inspired robot, drawing on the silkmoth’s odour-sensing abilities, received the Circular Economy Smart Reduction Award (循環經濟智慧減量獎) from the Environmental Protection Department, New Taipei City Government.

25 June 2026

Moth-Inspired Robot Places Fifth in Biomimetic Robotics

#awards #moth #robot

The lab’s silk moth-inspired robot placed fifth in the Biomimetic Robotics category at the 2026 Bionic Design Competition (仿生設計競賽). Inspired by the silkmoth’s odour-sensing abilities, the robot explores bio-inspired approaches to robotic sensing.

10 June 2026

Lab Teams Receive Honours at Engineering Research Project Competition

#awards #capstone #drone #flapping-propulsion #ray #robot

Two student teams from the lab received Honourable Mentions (佳作) at the College of Engineering Undergraduate Research Project Competition (專題研究成果競賽), with projects on an underwater bio-inspired stingray vehicle and a maple-seed-inspired unmanned aerial vehicle.
