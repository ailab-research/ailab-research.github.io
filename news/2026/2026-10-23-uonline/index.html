Lab Team Interviewed for Bio-Inspired Vehicle Research

#capstone #flapping-propulsion #interview #mudskipper #ray #robot

The laboratory team was interviewed by Uonline News (大學報) at National Chengchi University (NCCU) about their bio-inspired amphibious unmanned vehicles modelled after black ghost knifefish and mudskippers. Their award-winning designs attracted media attention during a showcase at a biomimicry competition.
